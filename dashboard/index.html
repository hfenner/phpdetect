How to read these results

Affected: the detector reports a matching affected vendor build. Patched: vendor package and running-build evidence are verified for that runtime. Unknown: evidence or coverage is incomplete. Host-wide clearance is never inferred from a subset of configured runtimes.

Lab fixture labels describe test intent only. They do not override discovery results. Discovery and remediation are separate playbooks; this dashboard performs no updates.
